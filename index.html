01
Solar Power Monitoring System
Arduino IDE · Proteus · Voltage & Current Sensors

A real-time embedded system to monitor the performance and efficiency of solar panels. Voltage and current sensors are interfaced with a microcontroller to continuously measure energy output. Processed readings are displayed on an LCD, giving instant visibility into panel health. The system flags drops in performance using threshold-based logic, helping users identify faults and optimize energy harvesting.

Measures voltage, current & power output in real time
Threshold alerts detect underperforming or faulty panels
Full circuit simulated & verified in Proteus before hardware build
Helps maximize solar energy utilization and reduce wastage
02
IoT-Based Fire Alarm System
ESP32 · MQ2 Gas Sensor · Arduino IDE · Blynk App

An intelligent fire detection system that uses the MQ2 sensor to identify dangerous levels of smoke, LPG, and carbon monoxide. On threat detection, the ESP32 triggers a local buzzer alarm and simultaneously sends real-time push notifications to a smartphone via the Blynk IoT platform — enabling remote monitoring from anywhere. The system is ideal for homes, labs, and industrial setups where early fire detection is critical.

Detects smoke, LPG & CO gas with MQ2 sensor
Instant mobile push notifications via Blynk dashboard
ESP32 Wi-Fi enables internet-connected remote monitoring
Local buzzer & LED provide on-site alarm indication
03
Automated Waste Segregation System
Arduino UNO · IR · Metal · Raindrop Sensors · Servo Motors · Proteus

A smart waste management system that automatically classifies incoming waste into three categories — wet, dry, and metallic — without human intervention. An IR sensor detects object presence, a metal sensor identifies metallic waste, and a raindrop/moisture sensor distinguishes wet from dry. The Arduino UNO then activates corresponding servo motors to direct the waste into the correct bin, reducing manual sorting and improving recycling efficiency.

Auto-classifies waste into wet, dry & metallic categories
Multi-sensor fusion: IR, metal detector & moisture sensor
Servo motors physically route waste to the correct bin
Circuit designed & validated in Proteus simulator
Promotes sustainable waste management & recycling
